Legal checklist

General information, not legal advice — check with the Rent Registrar / a lawyer. Rent law is provincial and changes.

  • Written tenancy agreement (e-stamp paper) — the landlord's responsibility to register with the Rent Registrar.
  • Registered with the Rent Registrar — without it, the Rent Tribunal will not accept applications.
  • Registered with the local police station / Police Khidmat Markaz (tenant details + agreement).
  • Keep every rent receipt — the record is the proof.
