Kiraya Register — کرایہ رجسٹر

For one house owner and his rent takers. The owner lists his houses and keeps every schedule — rent, gas, electricity. The tenant logs in, links with a house code the owner shares, sees the same bills, and proves each payment with a screenshot the owner approves. They chat in the app, like WhatsApp.

Honest limits — please read

  • This is a record + chat + approval tool — not a legal agreement and not a payment system.
  • A screenshot proves the tenant showed a payment. Only the owner, checking his own account, knows the money arrived.
  • Gas and electricity amounts are entered when the real bill arrives — the app never invents a utility figure.
  • No reminders when the app is closed. No real WhatsApp messages — the bell is in-app only.
  • Chat ticks mean “stored / fetched / opened in the app”, not WhatsApp delivery.
  • Photos live on the server disk until a persistent store exists; a redeploy can lose them.
  • The app never verifies an owner’s identity, a tenant’s identity, or a house’s ownership.
  • Legal lines are general information, not legal advice — check with the Rent Registrar / a lawyer.
